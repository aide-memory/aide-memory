# AIDE Memory Publishing Guide

Marketing launch strategy for AIDE Memory. This guide covers verified submission processes and URLs for major developer platforms.

---

## 1. Hacker News (Show HN)

**Platform:** https://news.ycombinator.com/

### Submission URL
- Direct submission: https://news.ycombinator.com/submit
- Show HN guidelines: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
- For context: https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html

### Process/Steps
1. Click "submit" in the top navigation bar
2. Paste your project URL in the URL field
3. Enter a clear title starting with "Show HN:"
4. Leave the text field blank (posts without URLs get penalized)
5. Submit and then add a comment explaining your project backstory
6. Engage with the community in the discussion thread

### Requirements & Tips
- **Title format:** "Show HN: [Clear Description]" - avoid marketing language
- **Content:** Provide a clear explanation of what the project is/does in your first comment
- **Username:** Use a personal username, not your company name (indicates you're a real person, not pure promotion)
- **Context matters:** Explain how you came to work on this and what's different/unique about it
- **Avoid marketing:** Drop any language that sounds promotional - it's an instant turnoff on HN
- **Visual demo:** If possible, the project should be immediately tryable in-browser

### Recommended Timing
- Post during weekday mornings (US time) for maximum visibility
- Avoid Friday evenings when engagement drops
- Best window: 9-11 AM PST weekdays

### Special Notes
- Read the full guidelines before posting
- One of the most high-engagement platforms for developer tools
- Community is skeptical of self-promotion; focus on technical merit

## 2. Dev.to

**Platform:** https://dev.to/

### Submission URL
- Create article directly: https://dev.to/new (when logged in)
- No external submission form needed

### Process/Steps
1. Log in to your Dev.to account (or create one)
2. Click "Create Post" or navigate to /new
3. Write your article in Markdown
4. Add tags relevant to your content
5. Upload a cover image
6. Preview your post before publishing
7. Click "Publish" (or "Save Draft" to review first)

### Requirements & Tips
- **Format:** Write in Markdown only
- **Tags:** Add relevant tags to increase discoverability (limit to 4-5 most relevant)
- **Cover image:** Include an eye-catching cover image (recommended)
- **Preview:** Always preview before publishing to check for grammar and formatting
- **Draft mode:** Use "Save Draft" if you want feedback before going live
- **SEO:** Include a descriptive title and clear opening paragraph

### Recommended Content Structure
- Technical deep-dive or tutorial works well
- How-to guides and case studies perform strongly
- Share your building process and lessons learned
- Link back to the full project

### Recommended Timing
- Tuesday-Thursday mornings (US time) for best reach
- Dev.to has strong organic discovery, so timing is less critical than HN
- Can republish "cross-posts" if originally published elsewhere (disclose this)

### Special Notes
- Dev.to is highly inclusive and supportive of indie projects
- Cross-posting from blogs is explicitly allowed if disclosed
- RSS syndication available for your posts

## 3. Console.dev

**Platform:** https://console.dev/

### Submission URL
- Email submission: david@console.dev
- No direct form; direct email contact required

### Process/Steps
1. Email david@console.dev with information about AIDE Memory
2. Include: what the tool does, why it's useful for developers, a link, and why you think it fits Console's focus
3. Wait for response (editorial review process)
4. If selected, Console will feature and review the tool in their newsletter and website

### Requirements & Tips
- **User focus:** Primary users must be developers
- **Built for developers:** The tool must be specifically designed with developer needs in mind
- **Self-service signup:** Must allow individual developers to try it without contacting sales/support
- **Quality:** Tool must be high-quality, do what it claims, and be actively maintained
- **Updates:** Demonstrate regular bug fixes and ongoing development
- **No payment required:** Console's editorial process is independent; they don't accept payment for inclusion

### Selection Criteria
- Featured weekly with 2-3 other developer tools
- Only tools meeting their criteria are selected
- Independent editorial review (not influenced by sponsorships)

### Recommended Timing
- Submit when you have a solid MVP and can support users
- Best if you have a clear unique value proposition
- Include metrics if available (users, adoption, etc.)

### Special Notes
- Console has ~1M+ subscribers for their weekly digest
- Highly respected by developer community
- Lower volume submissions but high-quality audience
- Very selective (editorial, not algorithmic)

## 4. Product Hunt

**Platform:** https://www.producthunt.com/

### Submission URL
- Prepare for launch: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch
- Submit new product: Click "Submit" button (top right) → "New Product"
- Help center: https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product

### Process/Steps
1. Log in to Product Hunt with your personal account
2. Click "Submit" button in top right navigation
3. Click "New Product"
4. Enter your product's URL as the primary link
5. Fill in product name, tagline, description, and category/topics
6. Upload thumbnail and gallery images
7. Schedule your launch (or launch immediately)
8. Post your maker's comment (first comment on the post)
9. Monitor engagement and respond to comments throughout the day

### Requirements & Tips
- **Product name:** Name only, no description or extra emojis (unless part of official name)
- **Tagline:** Short, catchy description (20-40 characters ideal)
- **Description:** Concise overview (~260 characters)
- **URL:** Direct link to product, not landing page or press article
- **Topics:** Select 1-3 most relevant categories
- **Thumbnail:** 240x240 pixels, eye-catching or professional logo
- **Gallery images:** Show product in action, UI screenshots, animated GIFs perform well
- **First comment:** Critical - include as maker; 70% of winners included one
- **First comment content:** Personal story, key features, roadmap, or special offer for hunters

### Recommended Timing
- **Best time:** 12:00 AM - 1:00 AM PST (midnight Pacific)
- Submitting early in the day increases visibility throughout the full day
- **Day:** Tuesday-Thursday typically see higher engagement
- Plan to be actively present for first 24 hours post-launch

### Launch Day Strategy
- Prepare your first comment in advance
- Plan to engage with all comments and questions
- Have a solid product demo/walkthrough ready
- Alert your community but don't spam

### Special Notes
- Product Hunt has "Product of the Day," "Week," and "Month" awards
- Voting happens throughout the day - early momentum matters
- Community is discerning; focus on authenticity, not hype
- Can link app store links and alternative platforms secondary

## Key Success Factors

1. **Authenticity**: Be genuine about what AIDE Memory does and its current capabilities
2. **Timing**: Coordinated launch across multiple platforms on same day/week
3. **Community engagement**: Respond to every comment in first 24 hours
4. **Clear value prop**: Answer "why does this matter?" in first 30 seconds
5. **Demo-ability**: Make it easy for people to try AIDE Memory immediately
6. **Uniqueness**: Emphasize what makes AIDE Memory different from competitors
7. **Follow-up**: Continue engagement beyond launch day
